HOW TO PLAY

Connect three marks in a row, column, or diagonal. First to three round wins takes the match.

Changing opponent, difficulty, or rules resets the match. Press Start match when ready.

A brief high-contrast frame every 3–4 strikes.

Auto is following your system’s reduced-motion preference. Choose Vivid or Extreme to enable animated effects, or Calm to keep them off.

Rules & controls

Draws give no points. The starting player alternates after every completed round. Restarting keeps the same starter.

Vanishing Marks: choose an empty tile. Your oldest mark leaves before your new line is checked. “NEXT OUT” shows which mark will leave on your next move. Three repeated positions or 60 moves ends the round in a draw.

Vivid adds lightning, rising electrical arcs, magma, and tension-driven shaking. Extreme intensifies the eruptions, branches, and tremors. Calm keeps the board steady.

Use Tab and Enter, or the arrow keys inside the board. Hard AI is unbeatable in Classic; Vanishing Marks uses a limited-depth search.
